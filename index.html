3. UX Rationale & Accessibility Commitments

Design considerations and standards applied throughout this interface system.

Accessibility First (WCAG 2.1)

Ensured text contrast ratios exceed 4.5:1 for normal body text. Built keyboard-navigable focus rings and aria-labels for screen reader support.

Clear Visual Hierarchy

Utilizes systematic font weights, color contrasts, and spatial scale to guide user focus towards primary actions and critical inputs seamlessly.

Tactile Micro-Feedback

Interactive active-scale transformations (active:scale-95) provide immediate feedback upon click or tap inputs.

Modal Dialog Component

Modals trap focus and prevent background scrolling to ensure users complete critical workflows without context switching.
